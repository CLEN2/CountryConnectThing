How to play
• Drag from one circle to another to connect them.
• Or click one circle, then click another circle.
• Each connection costs money and earns $10.
• Connection prices rise with progress and distance.
• Every connected circle earns $3/second.
• Every 25 successful connections earns a key.
• Home-country states start at $200 and get more expensive.
• Foreign states cost keys; distant countries cost more keys.
• Pinch to zoom; use the + and − buttons too.
